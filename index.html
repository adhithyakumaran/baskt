Dark Store Stock Asymmetry

Unlike e-commerce warehouses with central stock, quick-commerce operates hundreds of micro-depots. What is in stock in one neighborhood is often sold out or differently priced 2 kilometers away.

Stealth Surcharges & Small Cart Multipliers

Platforms adjust packaging fees, handling charges, and night/rain surge dynamically based on localized rider density. Baskt reconciles these before you commit.

Autonomous SKU Equivalence

If your preferred bread brand is sold out in one dark store, our semantic matcher locates equivalent grade-A whole wheat alternatives with identical gram weights.

Deep Commerce AI

Not just price comparison.

Comparing a grocery order requires multidimensional intelligence across pack sizes, live stock, and platform fees.

🎯 Cross-SKU

Product Matching

Cross-platform SKU canonicalization maps identical items despite disparate naming conventions.

⚖️ Unit Parity

Pack Size

Converts 450g vs 500g vs 1kg variations to standardized cost-per-gram metrics for fair comparison.

📦 Dark Store Stock

Availability

Verifies stock down to the specific micro-fulfillment center, preventing out-of-stock checkout dropoffs.

📍 Hyperlocal

Location

Resolves dynamic geofenced catalog boundaries that shift kilometer by kilometer across city blocks.

🏬 Batch Freshness

Store Inventory

Accounts for local batch expirations, SKU rotations, and freshness indicators across active pods.

⚡ Landed Cost

Platform Fees

Factors in small-cart fees, rain surges, night delivery surcharges, handling fees, and packaging charges.

🛒 Whole-Cart Math

Basket-Level Pricing

Optimizes the total order rather than isolated items, unlocking platform delivery fee waivers and bulk volume promotions.

Our Foundational Principle

"We compare the basket, not just the product."

Single-product price checkers fail because grocery shopping is a multi-item decision. Free delivery thresholds, handling fees, and mixed-item availability determine which platform actually wins.
